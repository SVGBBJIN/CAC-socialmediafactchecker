Coverage fell to 58% in 2023, agency says

The agency reported that coverage fell to 58 percent in 2023, its lowest level in a decade, according to figures published on Tuesday morning by the department responsible for the programme. Officials attributed the decline to a lapse in funding that began the previous autumn, and said the figure had been revised twice before publication without any public notice being issued at the time.
